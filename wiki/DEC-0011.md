## Context
Heuristic OCR-based symbolic recovery had grown a local provider label,
`symbolic-fallback`, distinct from the spec's allowed values.

## Decision
Rejected. `FormulaBlock.provider` is constrained to `unimernet` or
`ocr-fallback`; non-recovered cases are `FormulaFallback`. The label was
collapsed to `ocr-fallback` so all OCR-derived formula recovery shares one
contract-visible provider value. Do not introduce compatibility aliases or
broaden the contract locally. Enforced by RUL-0003.

## Alternatives considered
- Keep `symbolic-fallback` and add an alias map — rejected: two labels for one
  concept is exactly the contract drift CIF exists to prevent.

## Consequences
One provider label per recovery path; downstream consumers can rely on a closed
set of provider values.
