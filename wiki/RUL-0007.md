## Rule

Before any two layout providers' outputs are compared — or any comparison is
cited in a decision record — all three of the following must be **identical and
recorded in the run's `summary.json`**:

1. **Render spec** — the same `target_long_edge` and color mode reaching each
   provider ([[CON-0004]]).
2. **Detection threshold** — the same `threshold` and `formula_threshold`,
   applied at one place. A provider that applies no threshold is not comparable
   to one that does.
3. **Label taxonomy** — one shared label map. Providers must emit their **raw**
   `provider_label`; normalization happens once, client-side.

If any of the three cannot be matched, the comparison is invalid and must be
reported as such rather than summarized into counts.

An agent can check compliance by asking: *would swapping the provider be the only
difference between these two runs?* If anything else differs, the rule is violated.

## Rationale

Produced by [[DEC-0017]]. The pre-controlled page-6 comparison recorded
PP-DocLayoutV3 55 regions / PaddleOCR 51 / Surya 26 and was nearly used to settle
the layout standardization question. Review showed Surya's count was
**unfiltered** while the other two were filtered at `threshold = 0.5` /
`formula_threshold = 0.6`; that Surya's mean confidence was computed over values
defaulting to `1.0`; and that three divergent label maps decide independently what
counts as a `formula` — Surya maps `text_inline_math` and `chemicalblock` there,
PP has no equivalent.

The failure mode this prevents is specific and had already nearly occurred: a
model-selection decision, locked into the wiki, resting on numbers that measure
three different things. That is exactly the compounding wrong assumption the
anti-drift system exists to stop.

## Exceptions

- **Single-provider runs** (regression checks, threshold sweeps within one
  provider) are unaffected — nothing is being compared across providers.
- **Best-case mode** comparisons ([[DEC-0016]], [[DEC-0017]]) deliberately vary
  the render spec per provider. They are permitted, but must be labelled
  `mode: best-case` in `summary.json` and may not be cited as evidence of model
  quality — only of deployable performance.
