## Rule
`FormulaBlock.provider` may only be `unimernet` or `ocr-fallback`. When a crop is
not recovered into a formula, emit a `FormulaFallback`, not a `FormulaBlock` with
a novel provider string. Do not introduce additional provider labels or alias maps.

## Rationale
A second label for one concept (`symbolic-fallback`) is the contract drift the
CIF layer exists to prevent; a closed provider set lets downstream consumers rely
on known values. Produced by DEC-0011; related to the crop-only routing decision
DEC-0007.

## Exceptions
none.
