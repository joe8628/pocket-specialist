## Context
Early passes let native-text matching and OCR fallback consume `formula` regions
and synthesize formula content from heuristics. That produced premature, wrong
"formula semantics" and let page-level OCR fallback bypass real formula
extraction. PP-DocLayout also over-emits `formula` labels on dense prose.

## Decision
A layout `formula` region is **only** a crop proposal handed to the formula
stage. The pipeline routes the crop to UniMERNet, which translates it to LaTeX.
Native-text block matching must not consume formula regions, and `_native_contract()`
must not convert native/OCR text into formula semantics. OCR-region handling and
formula-region handling are split so page-level OCR fallback applies only to
text-like regions and never short-circuits crop-based formula extraction.

## Alternatives considered
- Infer formula content from native text / OCR heuristics — rejected: premature
  semantics, wrong output, and it masked missing real formula extraction.
- Trust raw layout `formula` labels as final — rejected: PP-DocLayout false
  positives stay in the 32–34 range per page across a 96–300 DPI sweep.

## Consequences
Architecture is now: layout proposes crops → UniMERNet translates → optional later
enrichment. Formula false positives become a model-quality/threshold tuning
problem isolated to the layout stage, not a contract corruption. Ties to the
fallback-label rule RUL-0003 and the native-coverage rule RUL-0004.
