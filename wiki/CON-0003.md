## Definition
A `PageLayout` is the persisted per-page artifact for degraded full-page OCR
pages, shaped `{"type": "PageLayout", "blocks": [...], "reading_order": [...]}`.
It preserves the page's identity as an ordered set of child blocks. The
aggregate `document.json` remains flattened CIF (`StructuredBlock` entries) for
downstream chunking and retrieval. The two roles are intentionally separated
rather than forcing one format to serve both.

## Why it matters / where it appears
Full scanned pages and image sources route through full-page OCR; flattening that
straight into CIF `TextBlock`s satisfied text consumers but lost the spec's §12
page contract. The dedicated page writer emits `PageLayout` for these degraded
paths only, while native no-layout PDF pages keep the simpler structured-page
writer. Full-page OCR normalization preserves the OCR `block_type` (mapped through
the shared routing helpers) instead of hard-coding `TextBlock`, and records
page-structured origin in provenance.

## Boundaries
`PageLayout` is the per-page artifact, NOT the document-level aggregate (that is
CIF in `document.json`). It is produced ONLY on degraded full-page OCR paths
(scanned PDF fallback, image sources), not on every layout-disabled page. A
`PageLayout` block is not a `Chunk` (CON-0001) — chunking is a later phase.
